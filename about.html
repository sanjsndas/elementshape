About the Journal

We write about the spaces that shape how we live.

Elements Shape Living is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design, residential architecture, and the materials that give form to daily life.

Our Mission

Good design is not a luxury — it is a form of care. It shapes how we rest, gather, work, and recover. And yet, so much of the conversation around design is either superficial or inaccessible. We started Elements Shape Living to change that.

Our mission is to publish rigorous, readable journalism about the built environment: the choices behind a well-proportioned room, the chemistry of a lime plaster, the economics of a passive house. We believe that understanding how things are made makes us better stewards of the places we inhabit.

We are reader-supported and editorially independent. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not publish articles to sell products. Every recommendation is earned.

Editorial Principles

What we stand for

01

Depth over speed

We publish fewer articles, but each one is researched, reported, and edited with care. We would rather be right than first.

02

Evidence, not taste

We explain the why behind the what — building science, material properties, historical context — so readers can make their own informed choices.

03

Design for everyone

We write for renters and homeowners, students and practitioners. Good design writing should not require a degree to understand.

How we work

Every article begins with a question — usually one we cannot answer ourselves. We then research, interview practitioners, visit projects where possible, and edit for clarity. Drafts are reviewed by at least two editors and, where relevant, by a subject-matter expert.

We are transparent about uncertainty. When the evidence is mixed, we say so. When a material's green credentials are overstated, we report that too.

Corrections

We take accuracy seriously. If you spot an error, please write to corrections@elementsshapeliving.com. We publish corrections promptly and transparently.
