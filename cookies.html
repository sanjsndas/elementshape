Cookies Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on Elements Shape Living, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 1 June 2026 Version 3.2

In short: We use a small number of cookies to make the site work, understand how readers use our articles, and remember your preferences. We do not sell your data. You can manage or withdraw consent at any time.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to site owners.

Cookies may be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (stored for a set period). They may be first-party (set by us) or third-party (set by a service we use, such as an analytics provider).

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels, which function in comparable ways. References to "cookies" in this policy include these technologies unless stated otherwise.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation — to keep the site secure, balanced, and functional.
  • Preferences — to remember choices you make, such as dismissing a notice or selecting a reading mode.
  • Analytics — to understand which articles are read, how readers navigate the site, and where we can improve.
  • Marketing (limited) — to measure the reach of our newsletter and occasional campaigns. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles or sell data to third parties.

3. Types of cookies we set

The table below lists the specific cookies we use, their purpose, and their duration.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
esl_session Necessary Maintains your session and keeps the site secure. Session
esl_consent Necessary Stores your cookie preferences so we don't ask repeatedly. 12 months
esl_prefs Preference Remembers reading preferences such as font size or dark mode. 6 months
_ga, _gid Analytics Google Analytics — measures traffic and reading behaviour anonymously. Up to 24 months
esl_analytics Analytics Our in-house analytics — records aggregate page views and referrers. 12 months
esl_campaign Marketing Measures the performance of newsletter sign-up campaigns. 30 days
mailchimp_* Marketing Set by Mailchimp when you interact with our newsletter forms. Varies

We may update this list from time to time as our services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies directly, and their use is governed by the privacy policies of the respective providers:

  • Google Analytics — for anonymous traffic measurement. Google Privacy Policy
  • Mailchimp — for newsletter sign-up and campaign measurement. Mailchimp Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash — images are served from Unsplash; they may set cookies for content delivery. Unsplash Privacy Policy

Where required by law, we obtain your consent before setting non-essential third-party cookies.

5. Managing your cookies

You have several ways to control or delete cookies:

  • Our consent banner — when you first visit, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.
  • Browser settings — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The method varies by browser; consult your browser's help section.
  • Opt-out tools — for Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent tool because the site cannot operate without them.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Where we rely on consent to set cookies, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For more information, please see our Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will notify you via a notice on the site or, where appropriate, by email. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this Cookies Policy or how we use cookies, please contact us:
